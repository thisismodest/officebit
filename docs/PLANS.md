# Plans

`src/sim/plans.ts`, with the games drawn by `src/render/play.ts`.

Friends make plans to do things together: at weekends and on bank holidays
from late morning, and on weekday evenings after work.

## What they do

| Activity | Who | Where | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| 🥏 Catch (a frisbee or a ball) | 2–4 friends | A clear spot of grass in a park | 1 h |
| 🧺 A picnic (days off only) | 3–6: friends, plus partners and children | A blanket laid out in a park | 1½ h |
| ☕ Catching up | 2–4 friends | Seats that `gather` (a diner booth) | 1½ h |
| 💻 Working on projects together | 2–4 makers | Seats that are a `worktop` (a booth, a park bench) | 2 h |

Park plans need daylight. A park is any room with `park: true` (the Green).
Any venue, or new furniture, joins in by having the catalog flags
([FURNITURE](FURNITURE.md)). The venue has to be open the whole time.

## How it goes

1. **Making plans.** At 08:00 each day, sociable people (or, for projects,
   ambitious ones) may suggest something.
2. **Who's asked.** Friends, and colleagues they get on with (affinity
   ≥ 0.1), so a colleague can become a friend. Makers are the people on the
   same venture, or anyone ambitious.
3. **Saying yes.** Whoever's asked says yes by how sociable they are and how
   well the two get on. They're only asked if they're free then: at home as
   their day has it, and not on a date. Nobody is in two plans at once.
4. **On the day.** They set off 45 minutes before it starts, and drop what
   they're doing at home to go. The organiser lays out the blanket, the
   frisbee flies round the ring, and laptops come out.
5. **Afterwards.** Time together grows friendships, at a quarter of a chat's
   rate. The blanket is packed away.

There are no plans on days the town has its own do: Bonfire Night, New Year's
Eve and Christmas Day.

The profile shows someone's plan ("🥏 Frisbee at the Green with Bea, 13:00").
The News reports when they get going.
