What's new

officebit is growing all the time. Here's what's changed in town lately, newest first.

0.4 · 1 October 2026

  • 🏡 officebit has a home of its own: officebit.town, with the town right on the front page.
  • 🚌 Buses! Eight stops round town, by the office, the shop, the school and people's homes, running both ways (stopping on either side of the road): hourly and a night bus too. Hop on to get across town, or walk if you'd rather. Add your own stops in the editor.
  • 🪧 Billboards by the highway and a poster at the bus stop, in glorious pixels. Tap one to see what it's for.
  • 🏗️ Already have a town? Crews come to put up what's new, like the billboards. Nothing of yours gets moved.
  • 👋 New here? A little welcome, and a ? to find it again.
  • ⏩ Jumping ahead is much quicker: a hundred days in under two minutes.
  • 🛣️ Tidier roads: kerbs round the ends of closes, and cars that keep to the road and to their own lane, go round turning circles and give way at junctions.
  • 👆 Steering someone? Tap a door, the stairs or a building and they go through.
  • ✏️ A tidier map editor: road, path, forecourt and zebra crossings share one button (hover over it, or press and hold, for the others), Esc puts things away, and edits save themselves as you go.
  • 🏷️ Name your town, from the cog: it shows at the top, so you know it's yours when you come back.
  • ☕ The diner and the shop open on time: staff no longer dither on the way in.

0.3 · 30 September 2026

  • 🥏 Friends make plans: frisbee and picnics on the Green, catching up at the diner, or a meal for three or four round one table.
  • 💻 Co-founders get their laptops out together, in a diner booth or on a park bench.
  • 📉 Startups can have good weeks and bad ones. They hire when they're doing well, and some close. Their people go back to their old jobs, or try again.
  • 🚗 Cars come in off any road for a drive round town, not just to stop and charge.
  • 🚚 Food trucks drive into town from any road and pull up by the pavement.
  • 🚶 People give way to each other and step aside to pass. No more walking through each other.
  • 🏃 Spot a friend across the street? People hurry to catch them up for a chat.
  • ⚡ A quicker town: bigger towns run smoothly, ready for more people to move in.
  • 📱 Play full screen, or add officebit to your home screen as an app.

0.2 · 30 September 2026

  • 🌅 The sun rises and sets for the real date, where you are.
  • 🎄 Christmas trees and fairy lights, New Year's fireworks, Bonfire Night, Halloween and the bank holidays.
  • ✏️ Edit anyone from their profile: their look, name, personality and job. You can even give them a baby.
